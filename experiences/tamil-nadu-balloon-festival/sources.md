## Check notes shown on the page

- The 2027 dates and venue are not announced yet. The 2026 festival was the 11th edition, 15-18 Jan, at the Jallikattu Grounds, Coimbatore.
- Children under 12 enter free; adults need an entry pass. Balloon display and tethered rides depend on the weather and the pilot's decision, and rides cannot be pre-booked.
- The organiser's site says the 2026 festival was organised with the support of the Department of Tourism, Tamil Nadu.
