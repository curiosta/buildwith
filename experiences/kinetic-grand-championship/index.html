03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Designing a human-powered amphibious sculpture that crosses roads, sand, mud and open water

Also useful:

  • Flotation, traction and drivetrain design (pedal power on land and water)
  • Following the 'Offishul Rules', including ACE status, self-sufficiency and safe conduct
Difficulty our estimateSkilled team
Prep time our estimate3–6 months of design, build and water testing

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. All forward movement must be human-powered over the whole course; for ACE status no batteries or stored energy may drive forward movement, and tyre or pontoon inflation must use human-powered pumps.
  2. Everything needed for normal operation (flotation, water propulsion, pumps, sand or mud traction aids, tools) must be carried on the sculpture all the way.
  3. Design for three terrains: wide tyres or traction aids for sand and mud, gearing for hills, and flotation plus paddle wheels or propellers for water.
  4. Safety rules prohibit projectiles, anchors, explosives and fireworks; fuel, compressed gas and lithium batteries must follow best practice (the rules point to Burning Man's flame-effects guidance).
  5. Read the engineering and art judging criteria early; the race rewards art and engineering as well as speed.
  6. Team roles: captain, pilots, pit crew; everyone signs the waiver and code of conduct.

Practise before you go

  • Read 'How to Build a Kinetic' and sketch your drivetrain and flotation.
  • Test flotation of your pontoon design in a pool or lake with the full pilot load.
  • Build stamina with long cycling rides, including sand or mud if possible.

Safety notes

  • Flotation must carry the full pilot load; test before race day.
  • No projectiles, anchors, fireworks or explosives; handle fuels and lithium batteries to best practice.
  • All team members sign the waiver and code of conduct.
