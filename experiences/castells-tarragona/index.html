01 · How to take part

Your way in

Go to an open rehearsal (assaig) of a local colla castellera and join the pinya; watch the Concurs, where the top colles compete (tickets by public draw).

Workshop / open build

Join the pinya at an open rehearsal

Colles castelleres are open clubs: anyone can join, and newcomers start in the pinya, the dense base that holds the tower up. Go to a colla's rehearsal (assaig); in Sep 2026 the Colla Jove Xiquets de Tarragona invited the public to take part in its open rehearsals.

Watch & buy local

Watch the Concurs de Castells

Every two years the 42 top-ranked colles compete in Torredembarra and at the Tarraco Arena Plaça in Tarragona. Tickets were allocated by public draw in 2026.

03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Pinya basics: where to stand, how to brace and when to push

Also useful:

  • Following the cap de colla's calls and the gralla music
  • Body safety: posture, the faixa (sash) and no jewellery
Difficulty our estimateBeginner (pinya)
Prep time our estimate1–2 rehearsals to learn a pinya position

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. Newcomers start in the pinya, the crowd at the base that supports the tower and cushions any fall.
  2. Push only when asked from the front; listen for the calls.
  3. Take advice from experienced castellers: everyone had a first time.
  4. The music of the gralla sets the rhythm the tower is built to.
  5. Rehearsals (assajos) are where you learn; colles practise weekly and the knowledge is passed on by practice.

Practise before you go

  • Read the CCCC's guide to castells and the pinya before your first rehearsal.
  • Contact a local colla and ask when its next rehearsal is.
  • Watch a performance first to see how the pinya works.

Safety notes

  • Follow the colla's instructions exactly; never climb unless asked.
  • Remove watches, rings and anything hard.
  • Children who climb wear helmets designed for castells.
