| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Newcomers start in the pinya, the crowd at the base that supports the tower and cushions any fall. / Push only when asked from the front; listen for the calls. / Take advice from experienced castellers: everyone had a first time. / The music of the gralla sets the rhythm the tower is built to. / Rehearsals (assajos) are where you learn; colles practise weekly and the knowledge is passed on by practice. / Read the CCCC's guide to castells and the pinya before your first rehearsal. / Contact a local colla and ask when its next rehearsal is. / Watch a performance first to see how the pinya works. / Follow the colla's instructions exactly; never climb unless asked. / Remove watches, rings and anything hard. / Children who climb wear helmets designed for castells. | Our summary of the resources below |
