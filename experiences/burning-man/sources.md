## Skills you'll need (section 03)

| Fact used | Source |
|---|---|
| Skills (this experience): Build safety for art, theme-camp structures and shade in high desert wind; Fire-art safety, with FAST approval and a burn licence for anything that burns or uses flame effects; Leave No Trace: MOOP control, burn-scar prevention, packing everything out; (Optional) Mutant Vehicle design to DMV criteria | Skills research, checked 2026-10-04 (IST) |
| Difficulty (our estimate): Some practice (art or vehicle builds: Skilled team); prep time (our estimate): 2–4 weeks of camp prep; 3–9 months for art or vehicle projects. Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures. | Curiosta editorial estimate |
| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Read the Survival Guide and the 10 Principles before you plan anything; Leave No Trace and radical self-reliance shape every build decision. / Engineer for wind: anchor shade and structures properly, brace tall pieces, and expect the organiser to ask for drawings and engineering for taller or climbable art. / Anything with open fire, flame effects or pyrotechnics needs Fire Art Safety Team (FAST) review and a BRC burn licence; liquid-fuel flame effects aren't allowed in camps or on mutant vehicles. / Never burn on the bare playa: burn barrels must be raised at least 6 inches, burn only unpainted wood, and burning art sits on a 4-inch layer of decomposed granite; remove ash and every nail afterwards. / Keep one ABC fire extinguisher per camp and per vehicle, and keep structures at least 20 ft from anything burning; fireworks and aerial flares are banned. / MOOP (matter out of place) planning: avoid feathers, glitter and loose decor, pre-cut materials at home to cut down on sawdust, sweep your site daily and on departure, and pack everything out. / Mutant vehicles: radically modified, lit at night, no faster than 5 mph, working brakes, guardrails on decks; DMV licensing required. / Read the 2026 Survival Guide PDF end to end and make a camp MOOP plan. / Pitch and stake your shade structure on a windy day at home; practise ratchet straps and guy lines. / Pre-fabricate and test-assemble your art piece at home; time the assembly and teardown. / Take a basic fire-extinguisher and first-aid course locally. / No fires on bare playa; raised burn barrels only; FAST approval for fire art. / ABC extinguisher in every camp and vehicle; no fireworks. / Anchor everything for high wind; light yourself, your bike and your art at night. / Leave No Trace: pack it in, pack it out, MOOP sweep. | Our summary of the resources below |
