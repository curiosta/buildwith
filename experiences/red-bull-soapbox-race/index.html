03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Designing and building a gravity-powered soapbox kart: chassis, steering, brakes, wheels

Also useful:

  • Building to the event's size and weight limits and passing scrutineering
  • Team showmanship: theme, costumes and a pre-race performance
Difficulty our estimateSkilled team
Prep time our estimate6–12 weeks of team design and build after selection

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. Read the current event's rules first: limits differ by city (LA 2026: max 6 ft wide, 12 ft long, 7 ft tall, at least 7 in ground clearance, max 176 lb without driver; UK/Ireland: 2 m × 3.5 m × 2.5 m, 10 cm clearance, 80 kg).
  2. Gravity only: no engines, batteries or stored energy. The pit crew (max 4) pushes only to the start line.
  3. Start with a stable, low chassis (plywood or a stripped trailer or ride-on mower frame) and keep wheels well spread for balance.
  4. Brakes and steering must genuinely work: brakes acting on at least two wheels (official guide), and steering that isn't twitchy at speed.
  5. Use sturdy wheels: thin pram or bicycle wheels can wobble or collapse over jumps.
  6. Make bodywork light and sacrificial (foam, cardboard) over a strong frame; the driver must be able to get out fast. Helmets are required.
  7. Judging covers creativity, showmanship and speed, so rehearse your skit as well as your driving.

Practise before you go

  • Sketch your design to the rules and build a small cardboard model to test the theme.
  • Build and test the brake and steering mechanism on a flat lot first, then a gentle slope.
  • Rehearse a 30-second team skit.

Safety notes

  • Helmets (and a gum shield in UK rules) for the driver and co-driver.
  • Working brakes on at least two wheels; test at speed before race day.
  • Driver must be able to exit the cockpit quickly.
