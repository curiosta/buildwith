| Difficulty (our estimate): Beginner (BYO boat: Some practice); prep time (our estimate): 1–2 build weekends plus a few crew runs. Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures. | Curiosta editorial estimate |
| Refresher, practice and safety steps: A BYO boat must fully enclose the four-person crew and must look like a boat; size, shape and construction are otherwise up to you. / Most teams build the frame from electrical conduit or plumbing pipe; keep it as light as practical. / No sharp edges: tape or cap all pipe ends and joints in case a crew member falls. / Practise running in step: agree a cadence caller and hold the frame at a common height. / You can also enter races with organiser-supplied boats, and solo entrants can be matched into teams. / Alice Springs in August: dry heat by day, cold mornings; hydrate and wear sun protection. / Mock up the frame with PVC pipe and fittings and test-run it with your crew on sand or grass. / Do short sand-running intervals in a group of four. / Watch the official YouTube channel to see how BYO boats are carried. / No sharp edges on the boat. / Heat and sun in the dry riverbed; hydrate. | Our summary of the resources below |
