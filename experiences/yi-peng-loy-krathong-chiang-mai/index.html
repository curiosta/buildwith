01 · How to take part

Your way in

Float a krathong free on the Ping River; DIY krathong workshops at ticketed events; lantern release only at licensed venues outside the city.

Workshop / open build

Make a krathong and float it

Make a banana-leaf krathong (DIY krathong workshops run at ticketed events) and float it on the Ping River, which is free.

Use local gear

Or buy one from local makers

Buy a krathong from a local maker. Sky lanterns may only be released at authorised, licensed venues outside the city.
