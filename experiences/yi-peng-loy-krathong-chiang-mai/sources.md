| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Start with a round slice of banana trunk (or bread or another biodegradable base) as the float. / Cut banana-leaf strips and fold them into pointed petals; pin them in overlapping rows around the base with wooden pins or toothpicks, not staples or metal pins. / Top with flowers, one candle and three incense sticks; keep it small and light. / Test that it floats level in a bucket before taking it to the river. / Float it gently from a safe, lit spot on the Ping River; don't lean over railings or step onto slippery banks. / Sky lanterns: release only at authorised events.
