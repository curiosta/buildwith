01 · How to take part

Your way in

Buy a costume from a samba school / reseller - the costume is your entry into the parade.

Use local gear

Parade in a samba-school costume

Buy a fantasia (costume) made in a samba school's barracão (workshop), from the school or a reseller. The costume is your entry: you parade in one of the school's wings (alas) through the Sambadrome.
