| Refresher, practice and safety steps: No sailing experience is needed: each ship has a permanent crew that teaches you the ropes. / Life on board runs on a watch system: each day is divided into watches, and a team of crewmates takes responsibility in turn. / Pack in a soft, foldable bag or rucksack, not a suitcase: there is no room for it by your bunk. / Trainees are mainly aged 15-25, with some older people; check the ship's own age and booking terms before you choose a voyage. / Ships range from Class C or D vessels with a handful of sleepers to Class A tall ships with 100 or more, and a voyage may be a Race Leg or a Cruise-in-Company: read the booking guide to see which suits you. / Read the 'A day on board' page so the watch routine is familiar before you join. / Work through the kit list and test-pack a soft bag. / Try a day sail or a taster session with a local sailing club if you can. / Follow the ship's crew and the safety briefing at all times. / Pack warm layers and waterproofs from the kit list. / Check each ship's own medical and age rules before booking. | Our summary of the resources below |

## Check notes shown on the page

- Pack in a soft bag, not a suitcase: space on board is tight.
- The 2027 series runs 8 Jul-1 Aug: Bordeaux 8-11 Jul, A Coruña 16-19 Jul, Sines 29 Jul-1 Aug.
