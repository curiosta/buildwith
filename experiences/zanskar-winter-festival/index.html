Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. The 5th edition ran 26-27 Mar 2026 at the Sports Stadium in Padum, Zanskar, organised by the Ladakh administration; the 2025 edition was on 20 Mar at Atting village.
  2. Programme items reported officially include snow skiing by the Snow Ski School Zanskar, cross-country, snow rally race, snowshoe race, snow yoga, snow sculptures, archery, volleyball, folk songs and dances.
  3. Stalls of government departments and self-help groups show local products.
  4. Officials describe harsh winter cold and a hard journey from Kargil to Zanskar; dates for the next edition are not announced.

Practise before you go

  • Read the 2026 opening and closing reports to see the programme.
  • Check the road and weather advice of the Ladakh administration before you plan winter travel to Zanskar.
  • Plan warm layers and altitude acclimatisation well before arrival.

Safety notes

  • Winter in Zanskar is severe: officials describe harsh cold and a hard road from Kargil. Check conditions and advisories before you travel.
  • Allow time for altitude acclimatisation.
  • Dates are not announced for 2027: do not book non-refundable travel before they are.

04 · Check before you go

What we could and couldn't confirm

Before you book

  • The 2027 dates are not announced. The 5th edition ran 26-27 Mar 2026 at the Sports Stadium, Padum; the 2025 edition was on 20 Mar at Atting village.
  • At the 2026 opening, the local councillor asked for the festival dates to be fixed well in advance.
  • Travel to Zanskar in late winter is hard: officials describe the harsh cold and difficult road. Check conditions and dates with the Ladakh administration before you plan.
  • Costs are not listed on Nomad. Check them, and the latest dates, on the official site.
