01 · How to take part

Your way in

Register as chase crew (online from mid-July; on-site daily 5:30-7:30am & 4:30-6:30pm at Crew Corral); Navigator volunteer apps Feb-Aug.

Crew

Crew a hot-air balloon

Register as chase crew (online from mid-July, or on site daily 5:30–7:30am and 4:30–6:30pm at the Crew Corral) and help launch, chase and pack down a balloon. Crew is unpaid.

Crew

Volunteer as a Navigator

Navigator volunteers commit to at least 5 shifts / 25 hours; applications ran 2 Feb–15 Aug in 2026.
