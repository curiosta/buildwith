03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Basic aircraft-building skills: sheet metal and riveting, fabric covering, wood truss wing ribs, welding and composite lay-up

Also useful:

  • Following an instructor in a hands-on workshop
  • Volunteer team roles at a large event
Difficulty our estimateBeginner
Prep time our estimatenone needed

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. Daily hands-on workshops cover sheet metal construction and riveting, fabric covering, building wood truss wing ribs, oxyacetylene and TIG welding, and composite lay-up.
  2. Workshops and forums are included with admission, and no reservation is required.
  3. Instructors, technical counselors, skilled volunteers and industry experts lead the sessions.
  4. EAA says more than 6,000 people volunteer each year, and that all interests, experience and skills are welcome.
  5. The 2027 workshop schedule is not published yet: check back.

Practise before you go

  • Read the workshops page to see which skills are taught.
  • Look through the volunteer pages to see which roles match your skills.
  • Plan which workshops to attend once the 2027 schedule is posted.

Safety notes

  • Wear sturdy closed shoes and eye protection when told in workshops.
  • Follow the instructor's directions around tools and equipment.
  • Late July in Wisconsin is hot: bring water and sun protection.
