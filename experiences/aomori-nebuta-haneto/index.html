Your way in

No registration: rent/buy a haneto costume, assemble at the haneto meeting point before your chosen float departs.

Dance as a haneto beside the floats

No registration: rent or buy a proper haneto costume (hanagasa hat and the rest), then gather at the haneto meeting point before your chosen float sets off. The giant illuminated nebuta floats are built by local nebuta masters.
