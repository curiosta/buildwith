01 · How to take part

Your way in

Guided artisan-quarter walks in the 3-4 weeks before Puja, then pandal-hopping; hands-on sculpting with an artisan not verified.

Watch & buy local

Walk the artisans' quarter, then go pandal-hopping

Join a guided walk through Kumartuli in the 3–4 weeks before Durga Puja to watch artisans finish the clay idols, then visit the pandals during the Puja. Walks are booked through event listings.
