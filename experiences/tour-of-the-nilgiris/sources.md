## Check notes shown on the page

- TFN 2026: pre-tour briefing 5 Dec, flag-off 6 Dec, final ride 12 Dec in Ooty. It is a supported tour, not a timed race.
- You must be able to fix a flat tyre yourself, and riders cannot ride after dark: the sweep vehicle picks up riders who miss a cut-off.
- One bag per rider (up to 65 litres). Some road and gravel bikes are available for rental.
