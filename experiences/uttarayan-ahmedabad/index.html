01 · How to take part

Your way in

Buy kites/reels at Raipur Darwaja Patang Bazaar, join a local family/homestay rooftop on 14-15 Jan; watch invited international kite makers at the IKF riverfront ground.

Use local gear

Fly locally made kites from a rooftop

Buy patang kites and firki reels from the makers at the Raipur Darwaja Patang Bazaar, then join a local family or homestay rooftop on 14–15 Jan and fly.

Watch & buy local

Watch the International Kite Festival

Invited international kite makers fly at the IKF riverfront ground on the Sabarmati. Entry for visitors is free; flying slots at the IKF are for invited flyers only (invitations go through Gujarat Tourism).

03 · Check before you go

What we could and couldn't confirm

Before you book

  • Avoid nylon or 'Chinese' manja (kite string): it is unsafe and restricted.
  • The official International Kite Festival 2027 dates are NOT announced yet; 14 Jan is the Makar Sankranti festival date.
