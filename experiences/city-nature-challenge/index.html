03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Photographing or recording wild plants, animals and fungi

Also useful:

  • Noting where and when you saw something
  • Helping to identify other people's observations
Difficulty our estimateBeginner
Prep time our estimate1 practice session

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. Anything wild counts: a plant, animal, fungus, slime mould or other evidence of life in your city.
  2. Wild means it was not put there and is not cared for by people; mark planted or tended things as captive/cultivated.
  3. Take a picture or record a sound, and note the location.
  4. Share your observations on iNaturalist or your city's chosen platform.
  5. A list of participating cities and a link to the global project will be posted in early 2027.

Practise before you go

  • Photograph a few wild plants or insects near home and practise noting the location.
  • Read the participate page so you know the rules before the weekend.
  • Check whether your city has joined once the list is posted.

Safety notes

  • Do not disturb wildlife for a photo.
  • Respect private property and park rules.
  • Do not pick plants or move animals.
