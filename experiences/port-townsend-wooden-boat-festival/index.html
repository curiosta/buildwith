01 · How to take part

Your way in

Buy a festival ticket, make something in the Woodworking Tent (open daily), join the workshops and get on the water in boats built by local builders.

Workshop / open build

Make something in the Woodworking Tent

The Woodworking Tent is open daily (9:30am–5pm in 2026): try a shave horse and drawknife, build a wooden pen, make and brand a keepsake or a cheese board.

Use local gear

Get on the water in wooden boats

Tour hundreds of wooden boats built by local and visiting builders and join the on-the-water activities.

03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Hand woodworking basics: shave horse and drawknife, simple joinery

Also useful:

  • Boat handling basics for small wooden boats
  • Reading a boat: hull types and how wooden boats are built
Difficulty our estimateBeginner
Prep time our estimatenone needed; one woodworking class helps

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. The Woodworking Tent is open daily with activities for beginners and experienced makers.
  2. You can try a shave horse and drawknife, build a wooden pen, or make and brand a keepsake.
  3. Workshops and talks run throughout the festival.
  4. On the water, follow the volunteers' instructions and wear a life jacket.

Practise before you go

  • Try a beginner woodworking class near you before the festival.
  • Read the festival's hands-on page and plan which activities to join.
  • Learn basic rowing or small-boat sailing if you want to get on the water.

Safety notes

  • Sharp hand tools: cut away from yourself.
  • Wear a life jacket on the water.
