| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Pick a kite that suits a seaside breeze: a tail or a stable sled or delta copes with gusts better than a light-wind kite. / Launch with your back to the wind; let line out and pull in as the kite points up; walk towards anyone you tangle with instead of yanking. / Give the demonstration arenas and big show kites a wide berth; fly only in public flying areas. / Wear gloves if your kite pulls hard; sunglasses and sunscreen on the open seafront. / The official site says the festival runs free workshops in kite-building and supervised flying sessions; check the next edition's programme on arrival. / Fly a sled or delta kite on a breezy Indian beach (Goa, Kovalam or Marina) to get used to gusty sea wind. / Build a simple kite at home using AKA's starter guidance. / Learn basic French kite words (cerf-volant, ligne, atelier) for workshops. / Stay out of demonstration arenas; keep clear of big-kite lines. / Wear gloves with pulling kites; protect against sun and wind. | Our summary of the resources below |
