Refresher

  1. Pick a kite that suits a seaside breeze: a tail or a stable sled or delta copes with gusts better than a light-wind kite.
  2. Launch with your back to the wind; let line out and pull in as the kite points up; walk towards anyone you tangle with instead of yanking.
  3. Give the demonstration arenas and big show kites a wide berth; fly only in public flying areas.
  4. Wear gloves if your kite pulls hard; sunglasses and sunscreen on the open seafront.
  5. The official site says the festival runs free workshops in kite-building and supervised flying sessions; check the next edition's programme on arrival.

Practise before you go

  • Fly a sled or delta kite on a breezy Indian beach (Goa, Kovalam or Marina) to get used to gusty sea wind.
  • Build a simple kite at home using AKA's starter guidance.
  • Learn basic French kite words (cerf-volant, ligne, atelier) for workshops.

Safety notes

  • Stay out of demonstration arenas; keep clear of big-kite lines.
  • Wear gloves with pulling kites; protect against sun and wind.
