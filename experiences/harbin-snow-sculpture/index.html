01 · How to take part

Your way in

Enter as a team (qualified sculptors only); everyone else visits Sun Island / Ice & Snow World.

Team entry

Enter a snow-sculpture team (qualified sculptors)

Teams of up to 4 apply in November with an A4 sketch (usual deadline 30 Nov). Group applicants need award certificates from earlier national competitions.

Watch & buy local

Visit Sun Island and Ice & Snow World

Everyone else visits the finished snow sculptures during the Ice & Snow Festival.
