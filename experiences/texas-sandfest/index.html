01 · How to take part

Your way in

Register solo/team/family in amateur categories.

Team entry

Sculpt in the amateur competition

Register solo, as a team or as a family in the amateur categories and build a sand sculpture in your own plot: a 1-day contest on 17 Apr or a 2-day open contest on 16–17 Apr 2027. Registration closes 11 Apr 2027, or earlier if plots fill.
