Your way in

Enter a team (usually universities and schools) in the Challenger, Cruiser or other classes, or volunteer as event crew along the route.

03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Solar electric vehicle design to the event regulations

Also useful:

  • Team management, logistics and support-convoy planning
  • Driving and race strategy over about 3,000 km of outback road
Difficulty our estimateSkilled team
Prep time our estimateone to two years of team design and build

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. Read the 2027 regulations and the team manager's guide before you apply.
  2. Classes include Challenger (pure efficiency endurance) and Cruiser (practical solar cars).
  3. Applications for entry for 2027 are open on the official site.
  4. Volunteers help run the event along the route.

Practise before you go

  • Start with a regional solar or electric-vehicle challenge.
  • Build and test subsystems (array, battery, motor) well ahead of the event.
  • Plan the convoy and outback logistics with the team.

Safety notes

  • Outback heat and remote roads: follow the event's safety rules.
  • High-voltage battery systems: trained team members only.
