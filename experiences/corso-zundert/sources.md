| Difficulty (our estimate): Beginner; prep time (our estimate): 30 minutes reading; optional 1 papier-mâché practice. Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures. | Curiosta editorial estimate |
| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Every hamlet builds its float in its own tent (19 tents across Zundert). Tents are freely open while builders work: weekday evenings from about 7 pm and Saturdays (in August also Sundays). / The official FAQ says everyone is allowed to help; walk in, introduce yourself and ask the hamlet what needs doing. / Floats are built as a steel frame, then shaped concrete-reinforcing wire, then about seven layers of papier-mâché; fine details use foam (tempex). Expect gluing, papering and cutting jobs. / In the last weekend the hamlets fix the dahlias (grown in their own fields) to the float, which is where many extra hands are needed. / Wear clothes you don't mind ruining, closed shoes, and gloves around wire and steel; follow the build leader's instructions on ladders and scaffolds. / Make a small papier-mâché shape over a wire mesh form at home. / Practise pinning or gluing flower heads (marigolds work) in tight rows on foam. / Learn a few Dutch phrases; most builders also speak English. / Gloves around wire and steel; closed shoes. / Follow the build leader on ladders and scaffolds. | Our summary of the resources below |
