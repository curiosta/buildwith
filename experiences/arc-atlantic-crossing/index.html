Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. This is an experienced-crew route, not a beginner one: the organiser requires the skipper and at least one crew member to take training.
  2. Training covers liferafts, man overboard, damage control, first aid, weather and emergency communications.
  3. Every rally boat needs at least two adults aged 18+: single-handers are not allowed.
  4. Everyone aboard needs an inflatable lifejacket with harness, tether and a personal AIS beacon.
  5. The crossing is about 2,700 nautical miles and takes around 18-21 days for the average yacht; ARC 2026 starts 22 Nov 2026 and ARC 2027 starts 21 Nov 2027.

Practise before you go

  • Build offshore miles on shorter passages with an experienced skipper.
  • Take recognised safety training before you look for a berth.
  • Read the safety-equipment page to see what the skipper must provide.

Safety notes

  • Trained crew are expected: do not join as a first-time sailor.
  • Check the skipper's safety equipment and experience before you commit.
  • Offshore sailing carries real risk: follow the safety requirements.
