Your way in

Register as a volunteer when 2027 registration opens; some positions need prior experience or certification, others are open to first-timers; trail positions are filled mostly by returning volunteers.

03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Cold-weather working: dressing for long outdoor shifts in arctic conditions

Also useful:

  • Logistics: weighing, sorting and loading straw, supply bags and food for the checkpoints
  • Race support: communications, checkpoint crew and front-of-house roles
Difficulty our estimateSome practice
Prep time our estimatea few evenings of reading

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. The Iditarod relies on a thousand volunteers across pre-race preparation, the Ceremonial Start in Anchorage, the Official Restart in Willow and the trail to Nome.
  2. Pre-race jobs are at a warehouse in Anchorage: bagging straw and sorting musher supply bags (the page says you need to be fit and able to lift 50 pounds).
  3. Some tasks need prior experience or certifications; others are chances to try a few jobs and see what you like best.
  4. Trail checkpoint positions are based on skills and experience, need a lot of physical activity, and are mostly filled by returning volunteers.
  5. 2027 volunteer registration opens in November 2026.

Practise before you go

  • Read the position descriptions on the volunteer page and note which fit your skills.
  • Build cold-weather experience: long outdoor days in winter clothing before you go.
  • Read the official pages on dog care and mushing terminology so you can follow what is happening.

Safety notes

  • Dress for the weather: outdoor roles run for hours in arctic conditions.
  • Follow the lead volunteer's instructions around dog teams.
  • Trail roles are physically demanding: apply only if you can manage long hours in the cold.
