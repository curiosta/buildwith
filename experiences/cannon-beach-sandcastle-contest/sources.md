| Difficulty (our estimate): Beginner to Some practice; prep time (our estimate): 2–3 beach or sand-pit practice sessions. Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures. | Curiosta editorial estimate |
| Refresher, practice and safety steps: One plot per entry; sand may not be added to or taken out of your plot. / Only water may be added: no paint, flour, sugar, starch, cement or glue. / Decorate only with things native to the beach, such as seaweed, driftwood and shells. / Pack wet sand well before you carve, and carve from the top down. / Keep your tools inside your plot, and clear them away to show judges you've finished. / Leave the sculpture for the tide; remove everything else. / Practise packing and carving on any beach or in a sand pit with a bucket and simple kitchen tools. / Read the organiser's how-to page and watch its live-demonstration link before the day. / Time a practice build to match the contest's build window. / Sun, wind and cold on an open Pacific beach: dress for coastal weather. / Watch the tide. / Fill holes and clear your plot. | Our summary of the resources below |
