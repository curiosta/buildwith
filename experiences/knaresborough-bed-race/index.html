03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Building or adapting a racing bed that passes scrutineering (size limits, no mechanical propulsion)

Also useful:

  • Team running and pushing over a 2.4-mile course including a river crossing
  • Theme decoration and parade etiquette
Difficulty our estimateSome practice (fit team of 6 plus passenger)
Prep time our estimate4–8 weeks of team fitness plus a few build weekends

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. Read the official rules: each team brings a bed decorated to the year's theme, an air horn, plus a helmet and lifejacket for the passenger.
  2. Decorated size limit: max 2 m wide, 4 m long, 3 m tall; decorations come off before the race, and the bed must have no mechanical propulsion. Scrutineers must pass it.
  3. A team is six runners plus one passenger (minimum age 10), with two adult marshals who attend a briefing.
  4. Everyone must be able to swim, because the course crosses the River Nidd; the passenger wears a helmet and lifejacket throughout.
  5. Keep left unless overtaking; no throwing objects or liquids; no alcohol for runners until after the race; finish within 30 minutes.
  6. Entries close at the end of February and a lottery picks the teams.

Practise before you go

  • Train as a team: interval runs pushing a loaded trolley or cart.
  • Check every runner can swim; do a pool session in clothes.
  • Build a test frame and check wheels and steering on rough ground.

Safety notes

  • All competitors must be able to swim (river crossing).
  • Passenger: helmet and lifejacket throughout.
  • No alcohol for runners until after the race; no throwing objects.
