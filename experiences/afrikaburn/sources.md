## Skills you'll need (section 03)

| Fact used | Source |
|---|---|
| Skills (this experience): Artwork structural safety (drawings and install/de-install plans scaled to height); Fire safety: contained fires only, extinguishers, burn approval and perimeters; MOOP and Leave No Trace in the Tankwa Karoo; (Optional) Mutant Vehicle design and DMV licensing | Skills research, checked 2026-10-04 (IST) |
| Difficulty (our estimate): Some practice (artwork or vehicle: Skilled team); prep time (our estimate): 2–4 weeks of camp prep; 3–6 months for an artwork. Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures. | Curiosta editorial estimate |
| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Register your artwork early; burns need approval, and placement or sound need registration. / Structural safety scales with height: under 1.5 m needs a diagram, materials list and install/de-install plans; 3–6 m needs a compulsory 3D model, construction drawings and a project timeline. Light your artwork at night. / No open, uncontained ground fires ever: use a fire drum or raised pit, never leave it unattended, and keep a serviced extinguisher visible in camp. / When burning structures, keep a safety perimeter of at least three times the sculpture's height (more in wind), marked with lights or tape. / MOOP includes glitter, feathers, cable ties, cigarette butts, ash, wood chips and even fruit peels. Prep materials at home, secure everything against wind, and bring magnets, rakes and bags. / After building, events and burns, clean your site and get it signed off by the MOOP/LNT team. / Mutant vehicles must be significantly modified, pass DMV testing and drive at walking pace (10 km/h max). / Draw your artwork with dimensions and a de-install plan; build a scale model. / Test-assemble and disassemble the piece at home; count every fastener so you can account for them on site. / Do a MOOP walk at a local park clean-up to train your eye. / No uncontained ground fires; extinguisher visible in every camp. / Burn perimeter of at least 3× the artwork height. / Desert heat, cold nights and wind: anchor structures; light artworks at night. / MOOP: everything you bring leaves with you. | Our summary of the resources below |
