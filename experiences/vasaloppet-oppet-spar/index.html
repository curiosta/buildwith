01 · How to take part

Your way in

Register online, choose a start time, and ski classic style only; about 12 hours to finish, with checkpoint time limits.

Learn the skill

Ski the classic 90 km

Öppet Spår Sunday follows the classic Vasaloppet course from Sälen to Mora, against nobody but yourself. Start groups go every ten minutes, and each checkpoint has a time limit.

03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Classic cross-country technique (diagonal stride, kick and glide)

Also useful:

  • Pacing over about 12 hours and feeding at checkpoints
  • Race logistics: bib collection, bags and bus transport
Difficulty our estimateSkilled
Prep time our estimatea season of ski training

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. It is a 90 km classic-technique race on 28 Feb 2027: the first start is 06:50, and groups start every ten minutes.
  2. You have around 12 hours to finish, and each of the seven checkpoints has a cut-off time; if you arrive too late you must stop and bus transport to Mora is arranged.
  3. Classic technique only: skating can lead to disqualification, and fluorinated ski wax is banned.
  4. You can take part from the year you turn 17.
  5. Bus booking opens at the end of November 2026.

Practise before you go

  • Follow a classic-ski training programme from the organiser's guides.
  • Practise classic technique and waxing on long tracks before race day.
  • Do a long ski of 30 km or more so the checkpoint time limits feel manageable.

Safety notes

  • Cold: dress in layers and protect hands, feet and face.
  • Contact checkpoint medical staff or an official if you need to stop.
  • Do not ski through the checkpoint cut-off times.
