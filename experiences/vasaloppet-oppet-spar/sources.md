## Check notes shown on the page

- You can race from the year you turn 17.
- Classic technique only: skating can lead to disqualification, and fluorine ski wax is banned.
- You have about 12 hours to finish; if you miss a checkpoint cut-off you must stop and are bussed to Mora.
