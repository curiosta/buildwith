Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. The 28-day Basic Mountaineering Course is the institute's foundation course for men and women: the FAQ lists it as designed for beginners with no prior experience.
  2. Seats are first come, first served; apply online, and arrive the evening before the course starts.
  3. A 10 km loaded-walk (15 kg) fitness test is held before you go to the mountain; those who pass continue.
  4. The institute recommends endurance training of at least 5 km with a 15 kg pack for a month before the course.
  5. Foreign nationals report to the Principal one day in advance with passport and valid visa, and must arrange their own insurance.

Practise before you go

  • Do loaded walks on uneven tracks for a month before the course.
  • Add stretching, breathing exercises and cardio; monitor your blood pressure.
  • Read the packing checklist in the institute's downloads.

Safety notes

  • High blood pressure: the institute does not recommend attending.
  • Follow instructors and safety protocols at all times.
  • Trainees can be debarred for substance or alcohol use or indiscipline that endangers others.

04 · Check before you go

What we could and couldn't confirm

Before you book

  • Age limits: 16-40 for most batches; 20-55 for the 18 Sep batch. One batch is for women only (23 May-19 Jun).
  • Foreign nationals report to the Principal one day early with passport and valid visa, and arrange their own insurance.
  • Arrive the evening before: the programme says late arrivals, even by one day, will not be accepted. A 10 km loaded-walk fitness test is held before going to the mountain.
  • Costs are not listed on Nomad. Check them, and the latest dates, on the official site.
