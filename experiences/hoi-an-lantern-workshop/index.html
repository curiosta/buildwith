01 · How to take part

Your way in

Book a 1-hr class (10am/2pm) then walk the lantern-lit old town on full-moon night.

Workshop / open build

Build a bamboo-and-silk lantern

Book a 1-hour class with a local artisan (10am or 2pm; booking via WhatsApp or Zalo), then walk the lantern-lit old town on full-moon night (the 14th day of each lunar month).

Workshop / open build

New: Hoi An International Lantern Festival (3–6 Dec 2026)

A first edition with lantern installations; a hands-on lantern-making area was reported by the Da Nang city tourism portal.
