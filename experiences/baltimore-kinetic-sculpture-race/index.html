01 · How to take part

Your way in

Build a sculpture with a team and enter it with AVAM (pilots and pit crew), or watch along the 8-hour route.

Build a fully human-powered art sculpture with a team (pilots plus pit crew) that crosses city streets, sand, mud and the harbour, and enter it with AVAM. No motors, batteries or stored energy.

03 · Skills you'll need

Skills you'll need

What to learn and practise before you go. Each resource is marked Official or Community-accepted and names its publisher. Difficulty and prep time are our estimate.

Core skill

Building a human-powered amphibious vehicle (pedal drive, floats, brakes)

Also useful:

  • Art and costume design on a working machine
  • Team roles: pilots and pit crew
Difficulty our estimateSkilled team
Prep time our estimateseveral months of team build sessions

Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures.

Refresher

  1. Sculptures must be human-powered: no motors, batteries or stored energy for propulsion.
  2. Water, wind, sun and gravity may help.
  3. Required safety gear on board includes a life preserver for each person in the water, working brakes, a tow ring and a tow rope.
  4. Inspections happen before the start, at the water entry and along the course.
  5. The route runs about 8 hours through streets, the harbour, sand and mud.

Practise before you go

  • Test flotation and steering in calm water before race day.
  • Ride the full distance on your machine at least once.
  • Rehearse the water entry with your pit crew.

Safety notes

  • Wear a life preserver in the water.
  • Working brakes are mandatory.
  • Heat and exhaustion over an 8-hour course.
