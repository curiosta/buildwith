| Skills (this experience): Cardboard boat building: hull shape, layered corrugated sheets, glued and taped seams; Waterproofing with a one-part coating (no epoxy or fibreglass); Paddling as a crew and getting out safely if the boat sinks | Skills research, checked 2026-10-14 (IST) |
| Difficulty (our estimate): Beginner; prep time (our estimate): 2–3 weekend build sessions (or none with an instant-boat kit). Difficulty and prep time are Curiosta editorial estimates for a first-timer, not organiser figures. | Curiosta editorial estimate |
| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Everything structural must be corrugated cardboard, including the hull, superstructure and seats. / Glue, caulk, tape and staples are allowed as secondary supplies; waterproof the hull with a one-part product only. / Design for a quick exit: every crew member must be able to get out easily if the boat sinks. / Boats are muscle-powered from inside the boat; no swimming or pushing from outside. / The course is a U shape of about 300 yards: round each buoy on the outside. / Life jackets and paddles are provided and must be worn and used; wear water shoes. / Build a small test hull from 2–3 sheets and float it in a pool or bath before the full build. / Practise paddling in rhythm with your crew. / If you can't build at home, plan on the instant-boat kit: about two hours to build on the day. / Wear the life jacket provided at all times on the water. / Design for an easy exit if the boat sinks. / Knives and box cutters: cut away from yourself. | Our summary of the resources below |
