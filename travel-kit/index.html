Travel hacks

Our tips: advice from experience, not rules. Check the official pages above for anything that matters.

  • Pack for the festival, not the city. Each listing's “Skills you'll need” section shows what you'll actually be doing, e.g. crewing a tall ship or crewing a balloon.
  • Desert burns (Burning Man, AfrikaBurn): read the organiser's survival guide, linked on the listing, before you pack.
  • Photograph new gear and its bills before you leave. It helps with customs on return and with insurance claims.
  • Split cash and cards between two bags, and keep a day's money in your pocket.
  • Download offline maps and a translation pack for the destination's language on home Wi-Fi.

Travel hacks

Our tips: advice from experience, not rules. Check the official pages above for anything that matters.

  • Rooftop kite flying at Uttarayan: bring sunglasses, a hat and finger tape to protect your fingers from the string.
  • Festival crowds (Durga Puja, Ganesh Chaturthi): carry a small cross-body bag and agree a meeting point.
  • Cold-weather festivals (Zanskar, Hornbill): pack thermal layers, and read the listing for any permits.
  • Set up your phone for local payments and maps on arrival, and keep some small notes for markets and autos.
  • Dress for temples: shoulders and knees covered, and shoes that slip off easily.
