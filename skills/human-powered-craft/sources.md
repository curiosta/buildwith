| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Get the current edition's rules before you sketch anything; limits change by city and year. / Power: gravity or human muscle only. Batteries and stored energy are almost always banned for propulsion (allowed for lights or music at Kinetic). / Karts: low stable chassis, robust wheels, brakes on at least two wheels, precise steering, quick driver exit, helmets. / Water: use materials lighter than water that don't waterlog (closed-cell foam, wood, sealed cans); test flotation with the full crew load; flotation jackets. / Can boats: seal cans, tape them into straight strings, bundle them with chicken wire, add a deck. / Frames you carry (beds, BYO boats): light conduit or pipe, capped ends, no sharp edges. / Plan for scrutineering, and rehearse your skit, because judges score creativity and showmanship too. / Build a scale model to the rules. / Test brakes and steering on a gentle slope. / Pool-test flotation with full load; check everyone can swim the distance the rules demand. / Helmets for karts and launches; flotation jackets for water events. / Swim requirements: Flugtag (100 yd), bed race (all competitors). / No stored-energy propulsion; no projectiles or fireworks. | Our summary of the resources below |
