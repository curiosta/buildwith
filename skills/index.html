03 · How we pick resources

Official first

  • Official resources come from the organiser, a government or tourism board, a national governing body or UNESCO. 122 of our 126 skills resources are official.
  • Community-accepted resources are used only when the event's community widely accepts them; each one says why.
  • Where no official resource exists, the page says so. We don't fill the gap with blogs, SEO guides, listing sites or commercial sellers. The full domain list is in allowed_sources.json.
