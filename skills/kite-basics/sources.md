| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Fly in steady, moderate wind in open space, away from trees, roads, power lines and airports. / Back to the wind; hold the kite by the bridle, let line out and pull in as it points up so it climbs; a helper can launch from 50 ft downwind in light wind. / Sinking tail-first means too little wind; diving or spinning means too much. Move the bridle tow point a little, or add tail for stability. / Walk towards another flier to untangle; never yank. / Gloves for pulling kites; never let line run through bare fingers. Plain cotton thread only at Uttarayan, never nylon or glass-coated manja. / Never fly in storms or near power lines; leave a kite that's tangled in wires. / Build: light but strong spars, a taut sail, a balanced bridle; start from a proven plan before inventing. / Fly a cheap kite on an open ground or beach; practise solo and assisted launches. / Build a sled kite from a bin bag and two sticks. / Practise untangling with a friend. / Power lines: never fly near them; abandon a tangled kite. / Line cuts and burns: gloves. / No nylon or glass-coated manja. / Sun protection; sunburn is the most common kite-field injury (AKA). | Our summary of the resources below |
