| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Sand: mix plenty of water in, stack wet 'pancakes' or pack forms in layers; a wider base lets you build taller. / Snow: start from a maquette, remove big masses from the top down, keep weight over a solid base; hand tools only at major contests. / Carve from the top down so debris falls on unfinished areas; keep sand moist with a spray bottle. / Clay: knead out air, build from the base up, score and slip joins, cover unused clay with a damp cloth, dry slowly in shade. / Eco rules for idols: natural clay and non-toxic colours; no PoP, plastic or thermocol. / Follow contest rules: e.g. SandFest allows water only (no glue or paint) and no power tools. / Beach or sand-pit packing practice. / Carve soap or floral foam top-down. / Model a small clay figure and let it dry slowly. / Sun and heat on beaches; cold injury in snow. / Fill holes after sand carving. / Sharp carving tools: cut away from yourself. | Our summary of the resources below |
