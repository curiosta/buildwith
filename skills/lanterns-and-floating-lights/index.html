Refresher

  1. Silk lantern: even the bamboo ribs on their rings before covering; glue sparingly and stretch silk taut; trim close to the rib.
  2. Use LEDs inside finished silk lanterns, not candles.
  3. Sky lantern: two people hold it upright while it fills with hot air; let go only when it lifts on its own; calm air only; never near wires, trees or roofs.
  4. Release only where it's legal: Pingxi has a defined zone and hours; in Chiang Mai, release only at authorised events (official rule page: Unconfirmed: no official source).
  5. Krathong: banana-trunk base, folded banana-leaf petals pinned with wooden pins; flowers, one candle, incense; float-test in a bucket; no foam, plastic or staples.
  6. Tie back hair and loose sleeves near flames; keep water nearby.

Practise before you go

  • Make a paper lantern on a skewer frame.
  • Fold krathong petals from leaves or paper.
  • Rehearse a lantern release with an unlit paper bag.

Safety notes

  • Open flames: calm air, clear surroundings, hair tied back.
  • Sky lanterns only in legal zones and at authorised events.
  • Biodegradable krathongs only.
