| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Silk lantern: even the bamboo ribs on their rings before covering; glue sparingly and stretch silk taut; trim close to the rib. / Use LEDs inside finished silk lanterns, not candles. / Sky lantern: two people hold it upright while it fills with hot air; let go only when it lifts on its own; calm air only; never near wires, trees or roofs. / Release only where it's legal: Pingxi has a defined zone and hours; in Chiang Mai, release only at authorised events (official rule page: Unconfirmed: no official source). / Krathong: banana-trunk base, folded banana-leaf petals pinned with wooden pins; flowers, one candle, incense; float-test in a bucket; no foam, plastic or staples. / Tie back hair and loose sleeves near flames; keep water nearby. / Make a paper lantern on a skewer frame. / Fold krathong petals from leaves or paper. / Rehearse a lantern release with an unlit paper bag. / Open flames: calm air, clear surroundings, hair tied back. / Sky lanterns only in legal zones and at authorised events. / Biodegradable krathongs only. | Our summary of the resources below |
