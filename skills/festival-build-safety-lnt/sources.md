| Refresher, practice and safety steps: Plan Ahead and Prepare is the first Leave No Trace principle: build at home, pre-cut materials, and know every fastener you bring. / Document structures to the organiser's height bands (AfrikaBurn) or engineering requirements (Burning Man); anchor for wind; light art at night. / Fire: approvals first (FAST at Burning Man, registration at AfrikaBurn); no fires on bare ground; raised, contained burn barrels; extinguishers visible; burn perimeter of at least 3× height (AfrikaBurn). / MOOP: no feathers, glitter or loose decor; tie down anything that can blow away; carry magnets, rakes and bags; sweep daily and on departure; get your site signed off. / Spectators at pyrotechnics: stay behind barriers, protect your ears, follow staff (Valencia fire service). / Pack it in, pack it out: everything leaves with you. / Read the Burning Man Survival Guide and the AfrikaBurn MOOP policy. / Test-assemble your build at home; time set-up and teardown. / Do a MOOP sweep of a local park. / No fires on bare ground; extinguishers visible. / Anchor everything; light art at night. / Leave No Trace: pack everything out. | Our summary of the resources below |
